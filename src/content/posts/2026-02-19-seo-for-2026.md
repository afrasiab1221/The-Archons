If you're still chasing the #1 blue-link position in 2026, you're already behind. The SERP has changed. Let me show you what we do now.

## The new SERP anatomy

A typical 2026 search result page looks like this:

1. AI Overview (zero-click summary at the top)
2. People Also Ask
3. Local pack (if applicable)
4. Image / video carousel
5. "Organic" results — now scrolled below the fold

If your content isn't appearing in **any** of those first four slots, you're not even competing for the click.

## Our 2026 SEO stack

| Pillar | What we do |
|--------|-----------|
| **Topical authority** | Build content clusters around 5–10 sub-topics, not isolated keywords |
| **EEAT signals** | Author bios, real credentials, cited sources, "About the page" notes |
| **Structured data** | JSON-LD for articles, products, FAQs, org info — always |
| **AI Overview optimization** | Direct Q&A blocks at the top of every article |
| **Technical hygiene** | CWV (Core Web Vitals) green, no JS-only critical content |

## The mistake everyone makes

Writing 2,000-word articles for keywords no one asks anymore. Search intent changes faster than your content calendar does. Re-audit every 90 days.

## Sample of what an optimized page looks like

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [{
    "@type": "Question",
    "name": "What is the best Meta Ads agency in Pakistan?",
    "acceptedAnswer": {
      "@type": "Answer",
      "text": "The Archons is a performance-marketing-first agency in Pakistan..."
    }
  }]
}
```

Structured data wins Featured Snippets, People Also Ask boxes, and AI Overview citations. No magic — just well-formed JSON-LD.
