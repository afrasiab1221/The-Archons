Most brands think they're "doing reels" because they post one a week. That's not a content engine — that's a single candle in a dark room.

## What an engine actually looks like

Three systems running in parallel:

1. **Always-on editorial calendar** — 3 hooks / week, shot on Mondays
2. **Trend intercept sprint** — anything that pops in your category, we shoot in 48 hours
3. **Long-tail library** — slow-burn content (recipes, family moments) that compounds over months

## The 3-step funnel

```
Reach    (top-of-funnel, broad hook)
  ↓
Follow   (mid-funnel, "save this" or "send to a mom")
  ↓
Convert  (retargeting, lead form, store visit)
```

Most brands only optimize for **reach** and wonder why the followers don't buy. We optimize for the entire funnel.

## What we shot for Milo

A few hits from our content engine:

- "Milo-and-yoga-morning" — slow-burn, family-first positioning
- "3 Milo recipes under 5 minutes" — search-friendly, long-tail
- "Pakistani mom's morning vs. global mom's morning" — trend intercept, high reach

## What the numbers looked like

| Metric | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Avg reach / reel | 12K | 80K |
| Saves per post | 40 | 320 |
| Followers / month | +600 | +3,400 |

That's not viral — that's compounding. The boring kind that pays the bills.
